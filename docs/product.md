# LUDI · Playable Yard Style Heritage

The approved painted SVG board, wood frame, deep green shell, bold condensed type and cream/gold controls are retained. Board orientation is fixed; the 52-cell geometry remains provisional.

Main Menu, Play, Local/Solo Setup, Online Create/Join, Lobby, Rules Studio, Game Table, Pause, Settings and Results are implemented as distinct viewport-sized screens. Desktop commands sit on the left; mobile commands sit under the board. Rules/setup/move lists scroll internally when needed.

Local supports 2–4 named human players and mixed human/computer tables with assigned colours. Solo supports one human and 1–3 basic computer opponents. Both work without the lobby server. Online uses real server-authoritative rolls, legal actions, timers, game state, winners and rematches. The host configures the table before starting; guests inspect rules. Started rules are locked.

Gold rings identify legal counters. Selecting a counter highlights its legal destinations. The command panel offers explicit dice allocations, captures, block formation and finishing choices without exposing technical identifiers. Two dice display the controlling runtime's results. Captures, block passage, home qualification, exact finish, bonus rolls and other enabled settings are enforced by the shared engine. Results and rematches occur only after an actual win.

Local pause suspends clocks and computers. Online menus do not stop other players. Exit requires confirmation. Sound preferences persist; reduced motion follows the device. Development tools are isolated behind an opt-in development-only inspector.

Rules and physical-board authenticity still have documented provisional interpretations. See game-engine.md, rules.md and board-references.md. No War, accounts, public matchmaking, durable online history or external AI services are in this build.
