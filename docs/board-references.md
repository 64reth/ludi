# Yard Style board research · 19 October 2026

Visual references inspected directly:

- [Ludi 4 Life gallery](https://www.ludi4life.com/pages/gallery).
- [Boards displayed against a brick wall](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/2479/0260/files/IMG_1605_480x480.jpg?v=1738250675).
- [A board in use at an outdoor table](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/2479/0260/files/IMG_0983_large.JPG?v=1508888104).

These maker photographs show dark raised frames, bold red/green/gold bands across cross-shaped tracks, illustrated or customised corner yards, small gate markings, and large central printed designs. Pieces are grouped in the corner yards in the table photograph. Frame construction/material species and finishing process cannot be established from these photographs alone.

Adopted visual cues: painted colour fields, banded arms, a substantial wood-effect rim, high-contrast gates, tactile ringed counters and a bold graphic centre. The four numbered colour sets, corner linework and centre L are LUDI design choices, not claims of traditional decoration. No photographic artwork, portraits or flags were copied into the application.

Structural evidence:

- [Ludi Don](https://ludidon.com/how-to-play-ludi) describes a conventional cross board for two to four players, coloured entry gates and home columns.
- [Ludi 4 Life rules](https://www.ludi4life.com/pages/how-to-play-ludi-jamaican-rules) describes four markers per colour, start gates, home triangles and a home-entry block condition.
- [Caribbean Ludi instructions](https://caribbeanludi.weebly.com/about-instructions.html) describes starting arrows and protected home paths.

The photographs establish visual variation, but their resolution and perspective do not verify every cell, the exact path count, or universal colour orientation. There is insufficient evidence to replace the existing coordinate map. The 15 × 15 cross, 52 shared cells, gate indices, five-cell home lanes, finishing regions and yard coordinates are unchanged. IDs remain stable. Geometry is explicitly provisional; the rendering is a reference-informed interpretation, not a certified universal Jamaican board.

Before gameplay, validate physical-board measurements, track count, gate/home transitions, colour arrangement and the exact home-block qualification with Jamaican players and makers. See rules.md for the separate rules research.
